DENTAL TREATMENTS

We provide a range of professional services to meet all your dental needs.

  • Basic Teeth Cleaning
    A routine cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, keeping your teeth and gums healthy.
  • Teeth Cleaning with Bicarbonate
    Includes a fine powder spray (bicarbonate) to deeply clean and brighten your teeth by removing surface stains.

  • Professional Teeth Whitening with Laser
    A laser-activated whitening treatment that brightens your smile in one visit.
  • Piercing
    Dental or oral piercing performed safely and hygienically by a professional.
  • Fillings (Grade 1 to Grade 4)
    Used to treat cavities or broken teeth. Grade 1 is a small filling; Grade 4 is more complex and covers a larger area.
  • Tooth Reconstruction with Pins
    When a tooth is badly damaged, we use tiny pins to support a stronger filling and rebuild the tooth.

  • Porcelain or Ceramic Crowns
    Natural-looking caps that cover damaged teeth to restore their shape and strength.
  • Zirconia Crowns
    Strong, durable, and very natural-looking—great for visible front teeth.
  • E-max Crowns
    A high-end ceramic crown with excellent aesthetics, often used for front teeth.
  • E-max Veneers
    Thin ceramic covers placed over the front of teeth to improve appearance.
  • Full Removable Dentures (2 Arches)
    A full set of removable teeth to replace all missing upper and lower teeth.
  • Metal-Based Partial Dentures
    A metal-supported denture for patients missing only some teeth, offering more stability.

  • Removable Braces
    Custom-made devices you can take out, used to straighten teeth.
  • Removable Braces for Both Arches
    A set of removable braces for both upper and lower teeth.
  • Clear Aligners (Transparent Trays)
    Nearly invisible trays that gradually move teeth into place—comfortable and discreet.

  • Zenex / Megagen / Straumann
    High-quality implants used to replace missing teeth. These are placed into the jawbone and act like real roots for crowns or bridges. Straumann is a premium Swiss brand known for exceptional quality and durability.

  • Tooth Extraction
    Removal of a tooth due to damage, infection, or crowding.
  • Wisdom Tooth Removal
    Extraction of third molars, which may be impacted or cause pain or crowding.
